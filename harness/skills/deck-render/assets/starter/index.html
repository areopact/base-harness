Appendix: research note

Evidence can be dense without becoming shapeless.

Question

State the precise research question, scope, and what would change the decision. Keep one claim per paragraph and distinguish observation from inference.

  • Population and period
  • Method boundary
  • Decision threshold

Finding

The body scale is intentionally close-screen. Use citations, narrow measures, and explicit uncertainty. A compact page still needs an entry point, hierarchy, and one highlighted result.

  • Primary result
  • Counterevidence
  • Confidence

Implication

Explain what the evidence changes, what remains unresolved, and where the detailed table or source document lives. Do not squeeze an entire study into the main narrative.

  • Action
  • Owner
  • Next check
