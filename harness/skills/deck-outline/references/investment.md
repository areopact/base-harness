# Investment outline mode

Use this reference only for fundraising and investor-facing narrative work.

## Sources

Structural priors come from the user's own reference material when supplied (a prior deck, an investor question list, a stage-appropriate template). Reference material describes what other decks contained; the outline never claims causation from structure alone and never lets a reference supply the company's content.

## Evidence law

Build the evidence table before the slide map:

| Claim | Value | Source | Freshness | Status | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Status is `VERIFIED`, `UNVERIFIED`, or `STALE`. Preserve unit and time window with every number. Metrics older than their decision shelf life are stale. An unverified claim may remain as a named gap in the outline, but never as slide-ready fact. Inventing a metric is a blocking defect.

## Narrative structure

Use the standard investor questions (problem, solution, why now, market, product, traction, business model, competition, team, financials, ask) as coverage, not as a slide quota. Record every question as `DEDICATED`, `FOLDED`, `APPENDIX`, or `SKIPPED` with a reason. A folded answer is valid; an untracked question is a defect. Adjust order to the company's actual strength and stage instead of copying a generic sequence.

For each slide include one assertion, evidence rows, audience objection addressed, visual proof, timing, speaker cue, and appendix link if needed. Keep the ask and next step explicit. The per-slide visual suggestion and any representation or claim guard become binding input to rendering.

## Gate

Approval covers the narrative arc, slide messages, coverage ledger, and visible evidence gaps. Rendering may start only after approval. No outline is ship-ready while an unverified metric remains presented as fact.
